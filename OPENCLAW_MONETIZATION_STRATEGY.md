## Risk Mitigation

**Risks:**
1. **Technical complexity** — Users may find setup difficult
   - Solution: Offer setup service, templates, detailed docs

2. **Reliability concerns** — AI might make mistakes
   - Solution: Human oversight, approval gates, safety checks

3. **Competition** — Other automation tools exist
   - Solution: Focus on autonomy, self-healing, 24/7 operation

4. **Scalability** — Managing many clients
   - Solution: Automate onboarding, use multi-tenant architecture
