**Description:** Track competitors, market trends, and opportunities.

### Direct Competitors
- Print-on-demand platforms
- Research newsletter authors
- Investment fund managers
- Crypto analysis accounts

### Market Trends
- Pricing trends
- Feature gaps
- User sentiment
- Emerging niches
