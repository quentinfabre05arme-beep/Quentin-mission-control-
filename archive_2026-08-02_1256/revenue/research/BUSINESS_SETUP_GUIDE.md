## 🎯 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (Based on Live Research)

**Key Finding:** For digital products, Gumroad and Etsy cost ALMOST THE SAME — you keep ~$87 per $100 sale on BOTH platforms. The real difference is TRAFFIC, not fees.

**Bottom Line:**
- **No audience?** → Start with Etsy (94M buyers built-in)
- **Have audience/email list?** → Start with Gumroad (lower friction)
- **Best strategy?** → Use BOTH (Etsy for discovery, Gumroad for repeat customers)

## 💶 TAX IMPLICATIONS (France)

### Digital Products to EU Customers

| Customer | VAT Rate | Who Handles |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| France | 20% | Etsy/Gumroad* |
| EU (not France) | Customer's rate | Platform* |
| Outside EU | 0% | You |

*Platforms handle VAT automatically if they are "deemed supplier"

### Micro-Entreprise Income Tax

| Revenue | Deduction | Taxable Income |
|---------|-----------|----------------|
| €2,000/month | 50% (services) or 71% (merchandise) | €1,000 or €580 |
| Social charges | ~22% of revenue | €440/month |
| **Net after charges** | | **~€1,100-1,500/month** |
